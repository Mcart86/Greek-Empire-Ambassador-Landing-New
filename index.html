Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need sales experience?

Nope. We'll teach you everything you need to know.

How much time does it take?

As much or as little as you want. Most ambassadors fit it around their existing schedules — a few hours a week.

Who can I work with?

Student clubs, sports teams, honor societies, performing arts groups, cultural organizations, campus events, academic organizations, and more.

How do I get paid?

Every time an organization you bring in places an order with Greek Empire, you earn. No order cap, no ceiling.

Is there a limit to how much I can earn?

No. Your earning potential grows with the number of organizations you connect.

What will I gain from this?

Real sales and marketing experience, new connections, flexible income, and a brand you'll be proud to rep.
