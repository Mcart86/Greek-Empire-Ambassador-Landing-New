Need to Know More?

Frequently Asked
Questions

Do I need sales experience?

Nope. We'll teach you everything you need to know.

How much time does it take?

As much or as little as you want. Most ambassadors fit it around their existing schedule — a few hours a week.

How do I get paid?

Every time an organization you bring in places an order with Greek Empire, you earn. No cap, no ceiling.

Who can I work with?

Any campus org — Greek life, club sports, student government, cultural organizations, honor societies, event committees, and more.

Is there a limit to how much I can earn?

No. Your earning potential grows with the number of organizations you connect.

What will I gain from this?

Real sales and marketing experience, new connections, flexible income, and a brand you'll be proud to rep.
